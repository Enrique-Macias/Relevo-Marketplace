# RLVO — Content and Marketplace Policy

Status: Initial MVP policy

---

# Purpose

RLVO is a marketplace for university communities.

Listings must be lawful, appropriate for the community and compliant with this policy.

RLVO may review, block or remove content that violates these rules.

---

# Prohibited products and content

The following categories are prohibited.

## Drugs and paraphernalia

Prohibited:

- illegal drugs;
- recreational drugs prohibited by applicable law;
- drug paraphernalia;
- content promoting their sale.

## Medicines

Prohibited:

- illegal medicines;
- controlled medicines;
- products whose sale through RLVO would violate applicable law.

## Weapons

Weapons are prohibited.

This includes listings whose primary purpose is to sell prohibited weapons or related restricted items.

## Alcohol

Alcoholic beverages are prohibited.

## Tobacco and vaping

Prohibited:

- tobacco;
- cigarettes;
- vaping products;
- similar restricted nicotine products.

## Stolen goods

Products known or reasonably suspected to be stolen are prohibited.

## Counterfeit products

Counterfeit products and unauthorized replicas presented as genuine are prohibited.

## Sexual content

Products or listings containing prohibited sexual content are not allowed.

## Documents and identification

Prohibited:

- official identification;
- institutional credentials;
- personal documents;
- documents that could facilitate impersonation or fraud.

## Digital accounts

The sale or transfer of digital accounts is prohibited.

## Animals

The sale or transfer of animals through RLVO is prohibited.

## Fraudulent tickets

Fraudulent, duplicated, invalid or misleading event tickets are prohibited.

## Violence

Content primarily promoting prohibited violent material is not allowed.

## Gambling

Gambling-related products or prohibited betting activity are not allowed.

---

# Contact information in listings

RLVO may restrict direct contact information inside listing titles or descriptions.

The intended contact mechanism is the product's controlled "Contactar por WhatsApp" flow.

This helps RLVO preserve the expected marketplace experience and moderation flow.

---

# Enforcement

RLVO may:

- block a listing before publication;
- send a listing to human review;
- remove a published listing;
- remove a profile image;
- warn a user;
- temporarily suspend an account;
- permanently suspend an account.

---

# Repeat violations

Between 3 and 5 repeated violations may result in permanent suspension depending on severity.

A severe violation may result in immediate suspension.

Enforcement is based on factors including:

- severity;
- context;
- repetition;
- risk;
- fraud;
- abuse;
- attempts to circumvent moderation.

---

# Automated moderation

RLVO may use automated tools to identify prohibited content.

Providers may include:

- OpenAI;
- Google Cloud Vision;
- Amazon Rekognition.

Automated systems may assist RLVO but do not replace all human review.

Cases requiring interpretation may be reviewed manually.

---

# Reporting

Users can report:

- listings;
- users.

The identity of the reporting user is not disclosed to the reported user.

---

# Appeals

Users may appeal enforcement actions through:

soporte@rlvo.com.mx

Operational response target:

up to 10 business days.

---

# Ownership and legality

Users are responsible for ensuring that:

- they are legally permitted to offer the item;
- the item is not stolen;
- the listing does not violate third-party rights;
- the description is accurate;
- the listing complies with applicable law.

RLVO does not independently verify ownership of every item.

---

# Policy evolution

This policy may be expanded as new categories, risks and marketplace functionality are introduced.

Any change affecting personal-data processing must also trigger review of the Privacy documentation.
